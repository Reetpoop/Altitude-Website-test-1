What we do

Four services, one mobilisation, one crew on the ground.

We bring a ground base station, a pure-water plant and a licensed flight crew. Everything below runs off the same setup, which is why combining them costs less than booking them separately.

Facade & glassSolar arraysSurface treatmentCondition capture
01Facade & glass

Pure water, controlled flow, no residue.

Full-elevation washing using reverse-osmosis and deionised water fed from ground level. Pure water dries without spotting, so there is no detergent film left to catch the next month of dust.

  • Reverse-osmosis and deionised water made on site, so the rinse dries clear without a squeegee.
  • Flow and angle managed per substrate — the setting for sealed glazing is not the setting for a tiled facade.
  • Exclusion zone moves with the crew rather than closing your entrance for the duration.
  • Overspray controlled by sequencing, with vehicle areas cleared elevation by elevation instead of all at once.
  • Hot water available where street-level or kitchen-exhaust elevations need degreasing.

02Solar arrays

Yield you are losing to dust.

Rooftop and podium arrays cleaned without walking the array. No panel loading, no micro-cracking from foot traffic, no detergent residue to bake on.

  • Nobody walks the array. No point loading, no micro-cracking, no cracked backsheets from foot traffic.
  • Deionised water only. Detergent leaves a film that attracts the next layer of soiling faster than no clean at all.
  • Output measured either side where your inverter data allows it, so the interval is set by evidence rather than habit.
  • Rooftop, podium and carport arrays, including the ones no safe walkway was ever designed for.
03Surface treatment

Buy back the interval between cleans.

Singapore's humidity and shaded elevations grow biological soiling back fast. Treatment applied on the same mobilisation as the wash buys you a longer interval before the next one.

  • Anti-mould and anti-algae on shaded and north-facing elevations where regrowth is fastest.
  • Applied on the same mobilisation as the wash, so you pay one setup instead of two.
  • Product named in the quotation, with the safety data sheet attached before we apply anything.
  • Test patch first on any substrate we have not treated at your building before.
04Condition capture

The facade record you get for free.

The aircraft is already flying your facade at close range. Capturing it costs almost nothing extra and gives you a dated visual record of condition, elevation by elevation.

  • Elevation-by-elevation imagery, dated and retained so next year's set can be compared with this one.
  • Defect log covering what we saw: sealant failure, drummy or cracked tile, corrosion staining, render cracking.
  • Flagged, not washed. Anything that looks loose gets recorded and left alone rather than blasted off your building.
  • Handed over as data, not only as a PDF, so it is usable by whoever does your next condition assessment.
This is not a statutory facade inspection
A BCA Periodic Facade Inspection is a separate exercise with its own methodology and must be endorsed by a registered Professional Engineer. What we hand over is condition evidence and a defect log — useful input to that process, and useful on its own for tracking deterioration between cycles. If you need the statutory inspection, we will say so and point you to it.
05Water system

The part that decides whether the glass streaks.

Water is made on site. A reverse-osmosis and deionisation stage strips the dissolved solids out of the mains supply, which is what makes a rinse dry clear instead of leaving a mineral ghost on the glass.

06Limits

When we stop, and what we decline.

What we will not do

  • We do not fly without a permit. There is no informal version of a CAAS activity permit and nobody should offer you one.
  • We do not wash loose or damaged substrate. If a tile is drummy it goes in the defect log and we work around it.
  • We do not claim to reach everything. Deep reveals, enclosed light wells and heavy mechanical soiling need a rope team, and we say so at survey.
  • We do not fly outside the agreed window to catch up a schedule, and we do not fly in marginal weather to avoid a return visit.
